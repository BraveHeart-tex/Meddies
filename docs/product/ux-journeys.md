# 02 - UX & User Journeys

## Experience principle

**Cozy Care** is the approved direction. Repo `DESIGN.md` governs visuals; [Approved UX and dose-state boundaries](#approved-ux-and-dose-state-boundaries) and the topic 03 [dose logging contract](./medication-domain.md#domain-and-dose-logging---approved-contract) govern interactions.

## Primary surfaces

| Surface              | Job to be done                                                                  |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Today / Home         | See today's scheduled doses, unresolved doses and the next action               |
| Medications          | See active medication records and remaining inventory                           |
| Add Medication       | Create a record manually; later assist entry by scanning a barcode              |
| Schedule Setup       | Assign recurring dose times to a medication                                     |
| Dose Action          | Confirm taken or skipped unambiguously                                          |
| History / Progress   | Review past dose outcomes without mixing scheduled and completed events         |
| Settings / Reminders | Configure reminder intensity, notification permissions and fallback preferences |

## Core journey - initial vertical slice

1. Install/open the PWA and add a medication using a manual name.
2. Define a recurring schedule.
3. See the resulting dose on Today.
4. Close the PWA and receive a reminder (once the delivery feasibility spike is validated).
5. Reopen the app from the reminder and confirm the dose as taken.
6. See the recorded outcome in history.

Barcode scanning and inventory/refills are later enhancements, not blockers for the initial medication + schedule + confirmation workflow.

## Design constraints

- Never make a decorative UI element ambiguous with a dose action.
- Preserve a distinction between Scheduled, Taken, Skipped and Not logged.
- Make notification permission limitations and offline state intelligible rather than silently promising reliability.
- Avoid reward mechanics that could misrepresent whether medication has actually been taken.
- Mobile-first, installable iPhone/Android PWA; keep essential interactions accessible.
- The primary device is the user's iPhone 17 (402×874 CSS px, DPR 3). Usable height is smaller because of Safari toolbars and safe areas, so content must scroll. Visual checks follow [verification](../verification.md#visual-verification).

## Approved UX and dose-state boundaries

- Launch is private, English-language and mobile-first, with an approved Google-account allowlist and no public registration. Today, Medications and History are the primary navigation destinations, with Settings in the header (#74). Visuals follow Cozy Care in repo `DESIGN.md`.
- Show scheduled, explicitly **Taken**, explicitly **Skipped**, and past **Not logged** distinctly. A past unconfirmed occurrence is never automatically described as missed intake. Reminder delivery or acknowledgment does not prove intake.
- Actual taken time and quantity are user-reported and distinct from planned due time. Provide correction flows without rewriting past scheduled expectations; see [03 - Medication & Dose Domain](./medication-domain.md).
- Setup and schedule changes require online server confirmation; dose recording and correction follow the [session contract](#session-contract) below. Never announce server success before acknowledgement.
- History export, selective deletion, account deletion and post-deletion expectations follow #12 below. History deletion must not silently reverse stock consumption.
- Use accessible semantic controls, non-color status indicators, reassuring copy and explicit privacy/permission limitations. Do not offer clinical advice or guilt-based motivation.

## Current online and session interaction contract - November 6, 2026

- Dose recording/correction updates affected Today/History views immediately, clearly marked saving until acknowledged. The server remains authoritative; reminders and stock are not confirmed by optimistic presentation.
- On definite rejection, restore the previous affected state and retain attempted details for retry. On uncertain transport/timeout, show that saving could not be confirmed, preserve the same action identity during the session, and reconcile safely. Conflicts require deliberate recovery, never silent overwrite.
- Pending and uncertain saves remain tracked across screen navigation/unmount, with recovery visible wherever the user navigates. New writes require connectivity; do not queue new offline actions or silently retry ambiguous writes as fresh actions.
- Cached content appears immediately on return and remains during refresh/failure. Refresh stale data on entry, focus and reconnect plus relevant mutation reconciliation; no routine polling. Local time advances due-time/day status without inventing unavailable data.
- Keep History date in the URL and preserve navigation context. Unfinished medicine/schedule and dose drafts survive app navigation in session memory; successful submission or explicit discard clears them. #92 explicit drawer dismissal rules still apply, including pending-save dismissal guards. Navigation never cancels a submitted save.
- No persistent device cache/queue or recovery promise after reload/app closure. A stable cold-launch skeleton is acceptable; skeletons otherwise appear only when no usable content exists. Available in-memory data is a possibly stale snapshot, not a promise of complete offline coverage.
- Sign-out/expiry clears private cached data, drafts and mutation state; prevent late requests repopulating them. Network loss alone is not sign-out.
- Cross-device synchronization and persistent offline viewing/writes are [deferred](./deferred.md). Server retry/revision/conflict safeguards remain. Installable PWA and closed-app push requirements are unchanged. Architecture: topic 06.

## #12 - Approved export and deletion scope (November 3, 2026)

## Approved decisions

| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D1  | Settings offers an online export of all retained server history, across all medicines, as a ZIP containing UTF-8 CSV files and a README. Include medicine identifiers/names, strength and quantity unit; scheduled occurrence identity/time, Taken/Skipped/Not logged state, actual intake time and quantity; unscheduled intake; retained correction history; unresolved server conflicts with both submissions clearly marked; and current stock, uncertainty, refills and count corrections. Include export time, schema version and Europe/Istanbul plus explicit timestamp offsets. Exclude future occurrences, deleted history, secrets, push endpoints and diagnostic/delivery logs. This is a personal copy, not an import/restore format.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Readable, complete retained history without exposing credentials or claiming recovery support. |
| D2  | Export is a consistent server snapshot. Attempt to reconcile the requesting session's in-flight/uncertain actions first; if unresolved actions remain, require explicit acknowledgement that their unconfirmed local state is not included. The export reflects server receipt at its snapshot cutoff, not unresolved optimistic state or other open sessions. Never present unresolved conflict submissions as confirmed outcomes. No offline export or silent partial download. Files already downloaded remain under the user's control after deletion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Separates the server snapshot from uncertain session work and conflict evidence.               |
| D3  | History deletion is online and deliberate: select one medicine or all medicines, then an inclusive calendar date range or all elapsed history. Select scheduled entries by scheduled date and unscheduled intake by actual intake date, in Europe/Istanbul. Preview exact scope and count, with a fixed server cutoff; final confirmation applies only to that preview and requires a fresh preview if affected records change. Include elapsed occurrences today, exclude future occurrences. Delete matching occurrences, outcomes, corrections and associated historical delivery detail; do not recreate them as Not logged. Preserve medicines, schedules and future reminders; cancel any remaining unsent dose/food reminders for deleted occurrences. Block confirmation while matching known pending actions or unresolved conflicts remain; explain reconcile/resolve first.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Predictable selection, no silent deletion of unresolved work or future routine.                |
| D4  | History deletion never reverses intake or changes current stock, uncertainty or the current low-stock episode. Remove deleted intake's detailed stock linkage and quantity/time trail; retain only the current stock baseline and minimal opaque replay/deletion markers needed to prevent duplicate effects or resurrection. Independent refill/count-correction history remains outside history deletion and is included in export. Deleted intake cannot later be corrected; use an explicit count correction for an inaccurate current count. Copy: "Delete these history entries? Your stock count will not change. This cannot be undone in Meddies."                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Privacy deletion without falsely putting consumed medicine back in stock.                      |
| D5  | Account deletion requires an authenticated online confirmation, warns to export first, and immediately disables access and future server processing. Report completion only once active personal data is removed: identity/session data, medicines, schedules, occurrences, outcomes/corrections/conflicts, inventory, preferences, device registrations, reminder jobs and delivery records, and any server-held export artifacts. A failed or interrupted cleanup remains visibly in progress and retryable with reminders disabled. Already submitted notifications may still arrive; downloaded files and notifications already on devices cannot be remotely recalled. Signing in again must not silently recreate the deleted account; fresh setup requires deliberate reauthorization.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Clear active-data deletion boundary without impossible recall promises.                        |
| D6  | Deletion is server-confirmed, not guaranteed erasure of a disconnected device. After acknowledgement invalidate deleted history in the requesting session; account deletion clears all private in-memory cache, drafts and pending mutation state. Other disconnected open sessions may still show stale in-memory data; do not promise remote erasure. On reconnect, validate account/deletion state before cache reuse or uncertain-action retry, clear deleted entries and reject stale requests for deleted history/accounts without changing stock. No persistent device queue or closed-app cache is implied. Show rejected work as not saved until acknowledged, without recreating deleted records. Keep minimal opaque deletion/replay markers outside ordinary restored snapshots, without medicine names, intake details, email or credentials, for as long as needed to reject old device actions and prevent restoration. Explain these limited markers separately from deleted personal content. Encrypted backups retain deleted content for up to 30 days; no instant backup purge or user undo. Before any restored service accepts traffic or sends reminders, reapply account and history deletions and replay protections. | Prevents stale devices or restored backups from reviving deleted data/reminders.               |

Marker storage, identifier choice, retention cleanup and restore mechanics need
verification in downstream implementation. Opaque markers may still be linkable;
do not describe them as anonymous or claim that absolutely no data remains.
No medication details or secrets belong in ordinary diagnostics. The 30-day
backup policy is unchanged. Account deletion removes refill/count-correction
history too; history deletion alone does not.
