Decisions

Three choices go beyond the FE-646 map. The maintainer accepted all three as recommended on 6 Nov 2026, and asked for each time row to put Time and Quantity on separate lines; the screens below show both.

D7

The saved handoff is the medicine's own page

After the server confirms a new medicine, replace ?add with ?medicine=ID. The page shows "Magnesium saved." with Set schedule and Done for now. Back from there goes to the list, never to a filled form, so a second save of the same medicine cannot start from history. Reload keeps her on the medicine, which still offers Set schedule.

Alternative: keep the handoff on the Add screen. Reload or Back then loses it and lands on an empty form.

D8

Kept drafts get a "Clear form" action

FE-612 keeps unsaved medicine and schedule drafts in memory across navigation, cleared on save or explicit discard. Page forms have no Cancel (FE-646), so there is no discard today. Show a quiet "Unsaved details from earlier" note with a ghost Clear form button, only when a kept draft is restored.

D9

Unit is a two-option toggle with nothing preselected

FE-646 maps two or three short, always-visible options to ToggleGroup, so Tablet / Capsule moves from Select to ToggleGroup. Today Tablet is preselected, so a capsule medicine can be saved as tablets by accident, and the unit cannot be edited later. Requiring a choice adds one tap and shows "Choose tablet or capsule." if skipped.

Product rules kept explicit

Strength and quantity

  • Strength describes the product ("200 mg") and is optional. Quantity is how much she takes at each time.
  • They never share a label. Quantity always carries the unit.
  • Supported quantities: whole or half tablets, whole capsules. No maximum in the contract; the design adds none.

Istanbul schedule rules

  • Times are 24-hour Europe/Istanbul, stated once in the form description.
  • Start date is today or later. Times earlier today than the save are not added, and the confirmation says when the first dose is.
  • 1 to 24 unique times; saved times read in time order.

Not in this design

  • No interval ("every X hours"), end date, pause, resume or edit controls.
  • No medicine editing. The medicine page says "Details and schedules can't be changed here yet."
  • No barcode placeholder (D1). No offline save.

Mobile and keyboard behavior

Phone

  • All targets 44 px or more; the primary is 52 px.
  • Keyboard open: the focused field scrolls above it; the save button stays in flow and is reached by scrolling (M8). No sticky footer to cover fields.
  • Safe areas: content clears the home indicator; Toasts sit above the nav.
  • 320 px and 200% zoom: days wrap 4 + 3 and long names wrap (S8). Time and quantity already sit on separate lines.

Focus and announcements

  • Add opens with focus on the heading, not the name field, so the keyboard does not cover the form on arrival.
  • Failed submit: focus the first invalid control; errors are linked through aria-describedby.
  • Confirmations use one role="status"; save problems use role="alert".
  • After a replace navigation, focus moves to the page heading; Done for now focuses the new list row.
  • Reduced motion: no transition between screens beyond the default.
