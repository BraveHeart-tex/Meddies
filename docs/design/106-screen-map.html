Rendered baseline

Screen rules

Page or drawer

Drawer for one decision about one existing dose: record, review, correct. It fits one screen with the keyboard closed, has one primary action, and its dismissal discards.

Page (route or query state) for anything that creates or changes setup: medicine, schedule, and later edits. Pages are deep-linkable, survive reload as a location, keep in-memory drafts across navigation, and need server confirmation to save.

No other drawers are proposed. Settings stays a page.

Header and action placement

Primary destinations: AppHeader with title, optional eyebrow and subtitle, at most one page action (pill Button) left of Settings.

Sub-screens: a Back row ("‹ Medications") above an identity header: tile, medicine name, strength · unit. Settings stays at the right.

Form end: one full-width primary Button size="lg" (page) or size="drawer" (drawer). Secondary actions are outline, Cancel is ghost, row actions are soft size="sm".

Medicine identity hierarchy

Same order everywhere a medicine appears: decorative MedicationTile → name (heaviest, wraps, never truncated) → strength · unit → routine or dose detail → status.

Strength describes the product ("200 mg"). Quantity is how much is taken ("1 tablet"). They never share a label, and quantity always carries its unit with correct plural.

Detail puts identity first and the routine second. Today and the drawer already do.

Dose anatomy (Today and History)

One Item row for both: scheduled time column → name / strength · quantity → DoseStatusBadge. The whole row opens the drawer. History drops its plain card and separate "Review dose" button.

Actual intake is its own line: "Taken 00:15 · Thu 8 Nov · 1 capsule". It shows the date only when it differs from the scheduled date. Skipped reads "Skipped · no intake recorded".

History "Not logged" rows are not interactive; earlier-date initial logging is separate scope.

Dates and times

24-hour HH:MM, Europe/Istanbul. Dates in copy use the picker's format, "Thu, 8 Nov", adding the year only outside the current year. ISO dates (2035-11-07) never appear in the UI.

The Istanbul note appears once per screen (Today footer, form description), not in every label.

Feedback placement

Field problems: under the field. Save problems: one Alert directly above the primary button, which changes to "Retry …". Load or refresh problems: one Alert at the top of the affected section, with one retry button, and loaded content stays below it.

One retry per problem. Remove the second "Refresh Today" when the refresh alert is showing.

Defects found in evidence (for implementation owners, not design decisions)
  • History quantity pluralization: "Omega-3 - 2 capsule".
  • ISO dates in the review drawer ("20:00 on 2035-11-07"), detail ("Starts 2035-11-07.") and setup.
  • Routine summary shows quantity as an unlabeled "(1)".
  • Saved handoff announces twice: "Magnesium saved." and "Magnesium is saved."
  • Native required on the time picker blocks submit before the app's own weekday error can show.
  • Settings header icon shows aria-current styling while no nav tab is current; acceptable, but confirm in FE-649.
  • Today with failed loading shows both "Retry loading medicines" and "Refresh Today".

Evidence and limits

What was rendered

Production build at commit b42cb86, Playwright Chromium, synthetic Auth.js session and fresh local Worker/D1, frozen Istanbul clock (Thu 8 Nov 2035, 14:02). 40 states captured at 402 × 874 and 320 × 740, including failure states forced by request interception.

The capture spec lived in a scratch folder and was not added to the repo.

Source only

  • Configured sign-in button, connecting, failed and access-denied states (fixture has no Google config, so it shows "temporarily unavailable").
  • Sign-out pending and failure.
  • App loading and error boundaries.
  • 400 / 401 / 409 save responses (409 covered by existing browser specs).

Not verified

  • 200% zoom, VoiceOver, keyboard avoidance and safe areas on a physical iPhone (FE-616).
  • Reduced motion in the live drawer.
  • The mockups above are static; they prove no runtime accessibility or behavior.
