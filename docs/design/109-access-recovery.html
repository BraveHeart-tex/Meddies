Settings, sign-in and session recovery

The edges of the private app: getting in with the approved Google account, Settings and sign-out, and what happens when a session ends in the middle of a flow. Built on the approved FE-646 map (D1 to D6), FE-647 (D7 to D9) and FE-648 (D10 to D14). Synthetic data, Fri 6 Nov 2026, Istanbul time.

Decisions for the maintainer

Five choices went beyond the earlier maps. On 6 Nov 2026 the maintainer accepted D15, D17, D18 and D19 as recommended and declined D16. D17 changes two approved screens (FE-647 "session ended" in the form, FE-648 F7 in the drawer); that change is recorded in both issues.

D15

Settings is a header page with no Back link

Settings opens from the header on every page, so it has no single parent and a "Back to Today" would be wrong half the time. Rule: a visible Back row appears only on sub-screens with one fixed parent (Add medicine, medicine detail, schedule setup). Settings keeps the bottom nav with no tab selected, and the header Settings button shows as current. Browser Back and the iOS edge swipe return to wherever she came from.

Alternative: a Back row on Settings that calls history back. It falls back to Today on a cold launch into /settings, which is the ambiguity this removes.

D16 · declined

No destination line on sign-in

Declined by the maintainer on 6 Nov 2026: the line adds little. Sign-in still returns to the intended page, including the History date, through returnTo; it just doesn't say so on screen.

D17 · conflict

One path for every session end

Expiry timer, a 401 from the session check on focus, or a 401 from any save all lead to the same place. Meddies confirms with /api/session, then clears private cache, drafts and pending attempts (FE-638), and replaces the page with sign-in "Your session ended" plus the return page. Private content and unsaved details are not shown after that.

Accepted. This changes approved screens: FE-647 "session ended" and FE-648 F7 kept the form details visible with "Sign in again". Under D17 those states last only as long as the session check (no extra screen). Alternative: keep the in-form alert until she taps Sign in again. It shows private content after the session has ended, which the FE-649 acceptance rules out.

D18

Sign-out warns only about unconfirmed saves

Sign-out is one tap with no confirmation. The exception is a dose or setup save that is still in flight or Not confirmed (D5, D13). Then an Alert names it and offers Stay signed in or Sign out anyway. The copy never says the save was cancelled. It says Meddies can't show the result until she signs in again.

Alternative: block sign-out until saves settle. An uncertain save may never settle in this session, so she could be stuck.

D19

Settings shows the session end, not the email

The session deliberately exposes no email or Google profile to the browser (docs/authentication.md). Settings says "Signed in with your approved Google account" and "Session ends Fri 13 Nov at 14:02", from the seven-day absolute deadline. That tells her when she will be asked again, which explains a sign-in a week later.

Alternative: expose the email to show "person…@example.test". That adds personal data to the client session for little gain.

Not added in this wave: reminder preferences (FE-566 push, FE-572 quiet hours, FE-573 snooze, FE-574 notification privacy), stock settings (FE-583, FE-589), export (FE-592), history deletion (FE-593) and account deletion (FE-594). Settings gets no placeholders for them; each backlog item designs its own entry later.

Which failure is which

Network and app failures keep her where she is. Only a confirmed session end or a refused account leads to sign-in. Network loss alone never signs her out.

Product rules kept explicit

Nothing private after the end

  • Signed-out screens show no medicine names, doses or counts.
  • After a session end or sign-out, drafts and Not confirmed attempts are gone; copy says so instead of promising they wait.
  • Reload and Home Screen launch obey the same boundary.

No claims about pending work

  • A save in flight at sign-out or expiry is "in progress", never "cancelled" or "lost".
  • She checks Today or History after signing in; the server is the record.
  • No offline queue or stored drafts.

Truthful sign-out

  • Sign-out clears this browser's session. It does not sign out of Google or other devices, and Settings says so.
  • "You're signed out" appears only after the server confirms.
  • No account email shown (D19).

Mobile and keyboard behavior

Phone

  • Targets 44 px or more; Continue with Google and Sign out 52 px.
  • Sign-in content starts below the safe-area top; nothing pinned to the bottom, so no home-indicator clash.
  • No text fields anywhere on these screens, so no keyboard handling.
  • 320 px and 200% zoom: Alerts and label/value rows wrap; buttons stay full width (S8, G6).

Focus and announcements

  • Sign-in with a reason: focus on the h1; the info Alert is read as a polite status once.
  • Failures: role="alert"; focus stays on the button.
  • After sign-in: focus on the return page's heading.
  • Sign-out pending: the button keeps focus and announces "Signing out".
  • Reduced motion: no transitions on these screens beyond the standard Button press.
