Today, dose recording, correction and History

The daily routine as one connected flow: Today shows what to do next, one shared drawer records, reviews and corrects a dose, and History shows each past day with the same row anatomy. Built on the approved FE-646 map (D1 to D6) and FE-647. Synthetic data, Fri 6 Nov 2026, Istanbul time.

Decisions for Bora

Four choices go beyond the FE-646 map and one proposed exception to FE-631 is listed separately. Bora accepted all five as recommended on 6 Nov 2026, including the D14 exception, which is also recorded in FE-631. The screens below show them.

D10

Group Today's rows by what she needs to do

Today has one list sorted by priority under "Today's doses · 3 of 7 logged". Split it into three headed groups: To log (Due now and Not logged), Later today (Scheduled) and Logged (Taken and Skipped), each with a count. The order stays the same as today. The headings explain why an 08:00 dose sits above a 07:00 one. Empty groups are hidden.

Alternative: keep one list and the "3 of 7 logged" count.

D11

History steps one day at a time

D6 already made the DatePicker navigate on its own. Add Previous day and Next day icon buttons (44 px) on either side of it. Next day is disabled on today. Every date change pushes /history?date=, so Back goes to the date she saw before and reload restores the date she is on.

Alternative: DatePicker only. Checking yesterday then takes three taps instead of one.

D12

"Now" means the minute she saves

The Taken form reads the clock once, when the drawer opens. If she keeps it open for 20 minutes, "Now · 14:00" still saves 14:00. Keep the label current: it updates each minute ("Now · 14:20") and saves the minute shown when she taps Save. Her other choices are never changed. If she meant the earlier time, she picks Other.

Alternative: keep the minute when the drawer opened and label it "When opened · 14:00".

D13

An unconfirmed save survives closing the drawer

FE-631: dismissing discards an unsaved draft. An uncertain save is submitted work, and FE-612 says to keep its action identity and details for the session. After an uncertain result she may close the drawer. The row then shows a dashed Not confirmed badge. Reopening shows the attempted details with "Retry saving as taken". Meddies also checks again on focus or reconnect. If the record arrived, the row turns into the real badge with one confirmation.

Alternative: discard the attempt on close and rely on refresh alone. She may then save the same dose twice with different details.

D14 · exception

Swipes that start on a form control don't close the drawer

Carried over from FE-631, where this was left open. Base UI dismisses the drawer when a downward drag starts on any element, including Amount, the pickers and the outcome choice. A small slip while adjusting the amount then discards the draft. Proposal: mark interactive controls data-base-ui-swipe-ignore="y". Swipes on the handle, header, summary and empty space still close the drawer, and Close, backdrop and Escape are unchanged.

This changes FE-631's approved behavior. Accepted by Bora on 6 Nov 2026 and recorded in FE-631. The drawer keeps the current behavior until FE-644 builds it.

Superseded wording in the FE-648 brief: it lists "confirmed success/Undo". D2 (accepted in FE-646) removed Undo: a confirmed dose shows a factual Toast with no Undo, and Correct outcome handles mistakes. These screens follow D2. The DESIGN.md wording update for D2 is still a separate follow-up.

Product rules kept explicit

Scheduled and actual stay apart

  • The time column is always the scheduled time. Actual intake lives on the badge and intake line.
  • Correction changes outcome, intake time or quantity. It never moves the schedule or the scheduled time.
  • Skipped records no time and no quantity, and the drawer says so before saving.

Nothing is inferred

  • No outcome is preselected from a row. Up next preselects only the one she tapped.
  • "Not logged" is never "Missed". No streaks, no exclamation marks.
  • Saving and Not confirmed never look like Taken.

Not in this design

  • No first-time logging for earlier dates: History Not logged rows are not buttons.
  • No unscheduled intake, no Undo, no stock, no reminders.
  • No offline queue or stored drafts.

Mobile and keyboard behavior

Phone

  • Targets 44 px or more; drawer primary 52 px; outcome choice 64 px.
  • Keyboard open on Amount: the drawer scrolls so Amount and Save stay above the keyboard (R5). No sticky footer.
  • Drawer bottom padding clears the home indicator; Toasts sit above the nav.
  • 320 px and 200% zoom: badges move under the title, Taken at options wrap, review buttons stack (T8, H7).

Focus and announcements

  • Every close returns focus to the opener. After a confirmed save, the row's heading.
  • Confirmation Toast: one polite announcement. Save problems: role="alert".
  • Rollover and background refresh are not announced; the drawer's content does not move.
  • History date change moves focus to the day heading and announces "Thursday 5 November, 7 doses".
  • Reduced motion: drawer fades instead of sliding; no row press scale.
